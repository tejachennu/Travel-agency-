Your first international trip is exciting — and a little nerve-racking. After planning hundreds of holidays from Gandhinagar, here's the checklist we give every first-time traveller, in the order you'll need it.

## 1. Passport (6–8 weeks before)

- **Six months' validity** from your arrival date and **two blank pages**.
- Name spelled **exactly the same** on passport and tickets.
- Renew early — appointments and police verification take time.

## 2. Visa and entry rules (4–6 weeks before)

Rules differ wildly. A few examples for Indian passport holders (as of October 2026):

| Country | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| Thailand | Visa-exempt up to 30 days + TDAC ([guide](/blog/thailand-visa-for-indians)) |
| Malaysia | Visa-free up to 30 days until 31 Dec 2026 + MDAC |
| Singapore | Visa via authorised agent + SG Arrival Card |
| Dubai (UAE) | Pre-approved e-visa for most ([guide](/blog/dubai-trip-from-ahmedabad)) |
| Vietnam | E-visa ([guide](/blog/vietnam-e-visa-for-indians)) |
| Bali (Indonesia) | Visa on arrival / e-VoA + Bali levy |
| Maldives | Free visa on arrival + IMUGA form |
| Nepal & Bhutan | No visa; Bhutan needs an entry permit and SDF ([guide](/blog/bhutan-trip-from-india-guide)) |

Rules change — confirm before you book non-refundable flights.

## 3. Bookings to keep offline

Save PDFs of your **e-tickets, hotel vouchers, visa, insurance and arrival cards** on your phone *and* print one set. Immigration officers often ask for return tickets and hotel bookings.

## 4. Money

- A **multi-currency forex card** for most spending.
- A little **local cash** for day one.
- Tell your bank you're travelling and enable **international usage** on one debit/credit card as backup.

## 5. Travel insurance

Buy a policy that covers **medical emergencies, trip delays and lost baggage**. It's required for some visas and invaluable everywhere.

## 6. Phone and apps

- An **international roaming pack** or a local eSIM.
- Offline **maps**, a **translator** app and your airline's app.
- Your travel agent's **WhatsApp** — ours is available 24/7 while you travel.

## 7. Packing smart

- Check your airline's **baggage allowance** (budget airlines are strict).
- **Medicines** in original packaging with a prescription.
- **Universal adapter** and a power bank (power banks go in hand luggage).
- Liquids in hand luggage: **100 ml bottles** in a clear bag.

## 8. At the airport

- Reach **3 hours before** an international flight.
- Keep your **passport, boarding pass and visa** in one place.
- Fill **immigration forms** honestly and keep your hotel address handy.
