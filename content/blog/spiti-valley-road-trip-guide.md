Spiti is the road trip people talk about for years — thousand-year-old monasteries, the world's highest post office, villages perched at 4,000 metres and the moon-shaped lake of Chandratal. Our Spiti travellers came back with the photos (and the Langza Buddha selfie!) to prove it.

> **The circuit:** Chandigarh/Shimla → Kalpa → Tabo → Kaza (2N) → Chandratal → Manali. **8 days**. Best from **June to early October** for the full loop.

## Why go clockwise (Shimla first)?

Entering through **Kinnaur** means the altitude rises **gradually** over several days, which is the best protection against altitude sickness. Leaving via **Kunzum Pass and Manali** gives you the dramatic finale once you're acclimatised.

## 8-day Spiti itinerary

1. **Shimla** — arrive via Chandigarh; stroll Mall Road.
2. **Shimla → Kalpa** — along the Sutlej into Kinnaur; views of Kinner Kailash.
3. **Kalpa → Tabo** — **Nako Lake** and the 1,000-year-old **Tabo Monastery**.
4. **Tabo → Kaza** — the cliff-top **Dhankar Monastery** on the way.
5. **Kaza circuit** — **Key Monastery**, **Kibber**, **Langza Buddha** and fossil village, **Hikkim** (world's highest post office) and **Komic**.
6. **Kaza → Chandratal** — over **Kunzum Pass** to the moon lake; camp stay.
7. **Chandratal → Manali** — via the **Atal Tunnel**.
8. **Manali → Chandigarh/home.**

## Permits

Indian citizens generally **don't need an Inner Line Permit** for this route; foreign nationals need one for the section near the border. Everyone should carry **government photo ID** for checkposts.

## Altitude and health tips

- **Hydrate** — 3–4 litres of water a day.
- **No alcohol** for the first days at altitude.
- **Ascend slowly** and keep a buffer day.
- Talk to your doctor about **altitude medication** before you go.
- Carry **warm layers** even in summer — nights are cold.

## Practical tips

- **Mobile network** is patchy; BSNL and Jio work in Kaza and some villages.
- **ATMs** are limited beyond Reckong Peo and Kaza — carry cash.
- **Fuel up** at Reckong Peo and Kaza.
- **Book an SUV with an experienced mountain driver** — this isn't a road for first-time hill driving.
