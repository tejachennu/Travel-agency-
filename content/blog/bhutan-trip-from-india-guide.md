Bhutan feels like nowhere else: prayer flags on mountain passes, giant dzongs above rivers and the cliff-hanging Tiger's Nest monastery. For Indian travellers it's also unusually easy — no visa, a fair fee, and a short flight or scenic drive from India.

> **Key facts (2026):** No visa for Indians · **entry permit** with **passport or voter ID** · **Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) ₹1,200 per person per night** (children 6–12 usually half, under 6 free) · licensed guide required beyond the border towns.

## Entry permit and documents

Indian citizens receive an **entry permit** — not a visa. You need:

- **Passport** (valid 6+ months) **or Voter ID card** — Aadhaar and driving licences are **not** accepted
- Hotel bookings and your itinerary
- A **licensed Bhutanese guide** for travel beyond Phuentsholing/Paro town areas

We process the permit and guide as part of our packages.

## What is the SDF?

Bhutan charges a **Sustainable Development Fee** to keep tourism "high value, low volume". **Indians pay ₹1,200 per person per night**, far less than the USD 100 charged to most other nationalities. Budget for it on top of hotels, transport and guide.

## Getting there from Gujarat

- **By air:** fly to **Paro** via Delhi or Kolkata (Bagdogra is another option, followed by road to Phuentsholing).
- **By road:** reach **Phuentsholing** from Bagdogra/Siliguri and drive up to Thimphu (about 5–6 hours).

## A 7-day Bhutan route

1. **Paro → Thimphu** — arrive and drive to the capital; evening at the Memorial Chorten.
2. **Thimphu** — Buddha Dordenma, Tashichho Dzong, handicraft markets.
3. **Punakha via Dochula Pass** — 108 chortens and Himalayan views, then Punakha Dzong.
4. **Punakha** — suspension bridge, Chimi Lhakhang and riverside time.
5. **Back to Paro** — Rinpung Dzong and a farmhouse dinner.
6. **Tiger's Nest hike** — 4–6 hours return; start early.
7. **Fly home** from Paro.

| Autumn | September – November | Best visibility, festival season |

## Tips

- **Dress modestly** for dzongs and temples — long sleeves, no caps inside.
- **Carry cash in rupees** (₹100 and smaller notes are easiest).
- **Acclimatise** before the Tiger's Nest hike — Paro is at about 2,200 m.
