The Statue of Unity at Ekta Nagar (Kevadia) is Gujarat's most popular weekend trip — and an easy one from Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar. Our travellers summed it up nicely: *"proper planning, nice hotels and yummy food."* Here's how to plan it well.

> **In short:** ~200 km from Ahmedabad · **closed on Mondays** (check the official site) · **book viewing-gallery tickets online** in advance · stay **one night** to catch the **laser show**.

## Getting there

- **By car:** about **3.5–4 hours** from Ahmedabad via the expressway to Vadodara, then Rajpipla road. Add ~30 minutes from Gandhinagar.
- **By train:** Ekta Nagar (Kevadia) railway station has connections from Ahmedabad and Vadodara.
- **Inside the campus:** park and ride on the official buses between attractions.

## Tickets: which one to buy

- **Viewing gallery** — takes you up to the gallery inside the statue for views over the Sardar Sarovar Dam and Satpura hills. **This is the one most visitors want**; weekend slots sell out.
- **Basic entry** — campus, museum and exhibition, without the gallery.
- **Express entry** — a pricier option with shorter queues.

Ticket prices and gallery hours are revised from time to time, so **book only on the official site** (linked below) and check the timings there.

## A relaxed 1N/2D plan

**Day 1 — Statue and laser show**
Leave Ahmedabad/Gandhinagar early, reach by late morning, check in and visit the **statue and viewing gallery**. Walk the **Valley of Flowers**, then stay for the evening **light and laser show**.

**Day 2 — Jungle Safari and Ekta Cruise**
Morning at the **Jungle Safari** zoological park and **Cactus Garden**, then a ride on the **Ekta Cruise** on the Narmada before driving home.

## Best time to visit

**October to March** is the most pleasant. Monsoon months make the dam and valley green and dramatic. Summer afternoons are hot — plan outdoor sights for mornings.

## Tips

- **Avoid Mondays** and long weekends if you dislike crowds.
- **Carry water, sunscreen and a cap** — there's a lot of walking.
- **Senior citizens and kids**: use the internal buses and keep the gallery visit early in the day.
- **Tent City or a nearby resort** makes the overnight stay part of the experience.
