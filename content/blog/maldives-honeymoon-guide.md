Waking up above a turquoise lagoon, snorkelling with turtles before breakfast and watching the sunset from your own deck — the Maldives is the honeymoon most couples dream about. The good news: with the right resort and meal plan, it's more achievable than people think.

> **Essentials:** **Free 30-day visa on arrival** for Indians · fill the free **IMUGA** declaration before flying · best weather **November to April** · **4 nights** is ideal.

## How Maldives resorts work

Each resort is its own island. You fly into **Malé (Velana International Airport)** and transfer by:

- **Speedboat** — resorts within about an hour of Malé; cheaper and flexible with late flights.
- **Seaplane** — scenic flights to more remote atolls; pricier, daylight only.
- **Domestic flight + boat** — for the furthest atolls.

## Beach villa or water villa?

A **water villa** is the iconic experience — glass floors, ladders straight into the lagoon. **Beach villas** are cheaper and often more private. Our favourite honeymoon trick: **2 nights beach villa + 2 nights water villa**.

## Picking the right meal plan

Food and drinks at resorts are expensive and there's nowhere else to eat, so the meal plan matters:

| Plan | Includes | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bed & breakfast | Breakfast only | Very short stays |
| Half board | Breakfast + dinner | Most couples |
| Full board | All meals | Relaxed stays, few excursions |
| All-inclusive | Meals + selected drinks & activities | Couples who want no bills |

Vegetarian and Jain meals can be arranged — tell the resort in advance.

## Best time to visit

**November to April** is the dry season: calm seas and clear water. **May to October** brings more rain and wind, but also lower prices and great surfing. Honeymoon peak is **December to February**, so book early.

## Our honeymoon checklist

- **Book flights to arrive early** if you're staying at a seaplane resort.
- **Ask for honeymoon inclusions** — many resorts add cake, flowers or a dinner.
- **Snorkel gear** is often free; bring a reef-safe sunscreen.
- **Carry USD** for tips and small purchases.
