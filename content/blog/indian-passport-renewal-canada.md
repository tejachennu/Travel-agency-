If you're in Canada on a work permit, study permit or PR card, your Indian passport is your most important document. An expired one can stall a PR application, a job change or a family trip home. The good news is that **Indian passport renewal in Canada** is routine. You apply online, submit at BLS International, and the High Commission or Consulate prints your new booklet locally.

> **The short version:** Fill the re-issue form on the **Global Passport Seva (GPSP) portal**, then submit the signed printout with your **current passport**, proof of **status and address in Canada**, photos and the fee at a **BLS centre in your jurisdiction**. Under fees effective **1 July 2026**, an adult 36-page re-issue costs **CAD 176 normal / CAD 349 Tatkaal** plus a **CAD 1.70** BLS fee. Allow **about 4 weeks for normal and under a week for Tatkaal** once the file reaches the mission, plus BLS and courier time. **Canadian citizens can't renew.** They surrender the Indian passport and apply for OCI instead.

## Who can renew an Indian passport in Canada?

You can apply through the Indian missions in Canada if you:

- hold an **Indian passport** (valid or expired), **and**
- have **legal status in Canada**: visitor, student, worker or permanent resident, **and**
- live in that mission's **consular jurisdiction**.

The Toronto Consulate says you can apply **up to one year before expiry** and should renew once **less than six months** of validity is left. Airlines and many countries refuse travellers with under six months left, so treat six months as your deadline.

**Not eligible:** anyone who has become a **Canadian citizen**. More on that below.

## Which mission covers you?

| Mission | Covers (check by postal code on BLS) |
| --- | --- |
| High Commission of India, Ottawa | National Capital Region, Ontario postal codes starting with K, Quebec postal codes starting with H or J |
| Consulate General of India, Toronto | Rest of Ontario, Manitoba, **Nova Scotia**, New Brunswick, PEI, Newfoundland and Labrador |
| Consulate General of India, Vancouver | British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Yukon, Northwest Territories |

Nova Scotia residents fall under **Toronto**, and BLS runs a **Halifax** centre under that jurisdiction. Families on the South Shore or in the Annapolis Valley don't need to travel to Ontario.

## Step-by-step: Indian passport renewal through BLS

### 1. Fill the GPSP form online

Indians abroad apply through the **Global Passport Seva (GPSP)** section of the Passport Seva portal. The MEA rolled out version 2.0 at missions worldwide in October 2025. Choose **re-issue**, then the reason: expiring or expired validity, pages exhausted, change of particulars, or lost or damaged passport. Enter your details exactly as in your current passport unless you're deliberately changing something, then print and sign the application.

BLS has posted notices in 2026 about portal upgrades and outage days when appointments had to be rescheduled. If the portal misbehaves, try again later rather than creating a duplicate application.

### 2. Prepare documents and photos

Use the **BLS re-issue checklist for your jurisdiction**. It's the final word, and it changes from time to time.

### 3. Submit at BLS

- **Walk-in or appointment.** Bring originals and self-attested copies. BLS checks your originals at the counter.
- **Post.** BLS has accepted passport re-issue applications by post as well as walk-in. It's slower, and your original passport is in transit both ways, so use tracked courier.

Since **5 April 2026**, OCI and visa applications in Canada have had to be made **in person** for biometrics. We haven't seen that rule extended to passport re-issue, but check the notices on the BLS homepage before you post anything.

### 4. Processing and police verification

The mission processes the file in its own system. Some cases are referred to India for police verification or to the office that issued your last passport. That is the main reason some renewals take far longer than others.

### 5. Collect or receive by courier

Your old passport is cancelled and returned with the new one. Keep it, because it holds your visa and entry history.

## Documents for an adult re-issue

- **Current Indian passport** (original) plus copies of the first and last pages, and the observation page if any
- **Signed GPSP application printout**
- **Proof of status in Canada**: PR card, work permit, study permit or visitor record. On maintained status? Add proof of your extension application.
- **Proof of address** in your jurisdiction, such as a driver's licence, recent utility bill or bank statement
- **Photographs** (see below)
- **Declarations or annexures** named on the checklist
- **For a name change after marriage:** marriage certificate and your spouse's passport copy

### Photo rules

BLS Canada's printed-photo specification is **2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm)**, colour, full face, eyes open, on a **plain white or off-white background**. The GPSP portal also asks for a digital photo with its own size and file limits. Follow what the portal shows on screen, because third-party guides disagree. Shadows, glasses glare and cream-coloured backgrounds are the classic reasons photos get rejected.

## Indian passport renewal fees in Canada (2026)

Indian missions in Canada revised consular fees from **1 July 2026**. Applications made with the old fee are not accepted.

| Service | Normal (CAD) | Tatkaal (CAD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adult re-issue, 36 pages | 176 | 349 |
| Adult re-issue, 60 pages (jumbo) | 246 | 419 |
| Minor (under 18), 36 pages | 128 | 301 |
| Adult, in lieu of lost/damaged, 36 pages | 349 | 522* |
| Minor, in lieu of lost/damaged, 36 pages | 301 | 474* |

Add the **BLS service fee of CAD 1.70** per application, plus courier if you choose it. Figures are from the Toronto Consulate and BLS fee tables as of October 2026. The 60-page normal fee is taken from the BLS Ottawa table.

*The consulate lists Tatkaal fees for lost or damaged passports, but a BLS Toronto FAQ says lost-passport cases can't use Tatkaal. Confirm before you choose it. **Tatkaal isn't available in every jurisdiction**, so check with your BLS centre too.

## Processing times: normal vs Tatkaal

| Type | Official guide (from receipt at the mission) |
| --- | --- |
| Normal re-issue | About 20–21 working days (Toronto), excluding police verification |
| Tatkaal re-issue | About 3–4 working days (Toronto) |
| Cases referred to India or another issuing office | Can stretch to around 2 months (Vancouver guidance) |

BLS handling and courier time come on top. If you have a trip home booked within six weeks, talk to BLS about Tatkaal or move the trip.

## Minors' passports

- **Both parents' consent** is normally given on **Annexure D**. Where only one parent can sign, **Annexure C** applies. The Toronto Consulate notes that self-attested annexures replaced notarised affidavits for minors in 2016.
- Bring **both parents' passport copies** along with the child's current passport, status and address proof.
- **Custody:** the Toronto Consulate says Canadian court custody orders aren't recognised for re-issue. You need an Indian court order or both parents' consent.
- **Validity:** minors usually get a **5-year** passport. Teenagers aged 15 to 18 can get 10 years by paying the adult fee.
- **Children born in Canada** are Canadian citizens by birth, so most families choose a Canadian passport and OCI. If you're considering an Indian passport for a Canadian-born child, the missions require birth registration first. Talk to us before applying.

## Expired or lost passports

### Expired passport

You can still renew. The Vancouver Consulate's rules are a useful guide. If the passport expired **more than a year ago**, add a self-declaration explaining the delay. If it expired **more than three years ago**, you'll also need a **search letter from IRCC** and an affidavit, which confirm you haven't taken Canadian citizenship. Other jurisdictions may differ, so read your checklist.

### Lost or damaged passport

Report a loss to the **local police** first and get a report that **mentions the passport number**. The Toronto Consulate's list for a replacement includes the police report, a letter explaining how it was lost, the affidavit annexure for a lost passport, status and address proof and photos. A replacement costs considerably more than a normal re-issue (see the table). It can also take longer, because the mission may verify your details with the office that issued the lost passport.

## Are e-passports issued in Canada?

India rolled out chip-based **e-passports** across India in 2025. The MEA moved its embassies and consulates worldwide onto **GPSP 2.0** on **28 October 2025** and has been rolling out e-passports to Indians abroad. Some missions, including in the UAE, announced e-passport issuance from that date. As of October 2026 we couldn't find an announcement from the Indian missions in Canada confirming that every re-issued passport is an e-passport. Your new booklet may or may not have the chip.

Either way, **existing non-chip passports stay valid until they expire**. Don't renew early just to get a chip.

## Became a Canadian citizen? Don't renew

India doesn't allow dual citizenship. Once you take the Canadian citizenship oath, your Indian citizenship ends and you **must not travel on, or renew, your Indian passport**. Doing so can attract penalties. Instead:

1. Apply for an [Indian passport surrender certificate](/blog/indian-passport-surrender-certificate-canada).
2. Then apply for an [OCI card](/blog/oci-card-application-canada). Since April 2026 that means an in-person BLS visit for biometrics.

If you're still an Indian citizen and need a police certificate for PR, renew your passport **before** applying. The missions won't accept a PCC application alongside a re-issue. See our [Indian PCC in Canada guide](/blog/indian-pcc-canada-bls).

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Wrong jurisdiction.** Atlantic Canada is Toronto, not Ottawa.
- **Paying the pre-July 2026 fee.** The file will be returned.
- **Photo not to spec**, or a different signature from your last passport.
- **Expired status documents** in Canada with no proof of extension.
- **Waiting until the last six months** before a planned trip.
- **Renewing after becoming Canadian.** Surrender and get OCI instead.
