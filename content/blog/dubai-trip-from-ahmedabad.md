Dubai is the classic first international holiday for many families in Gujarat — and for good reason. The flight from Ahmedabad is short, the city is safe and spotless, there's vegetarian food everywhere, and you can fit desert dunes, the world's tallest building and a beach into one long weekend. Our travellers keep telling us the same thing: *"perfectly balanced — iconic landmarks, but time to relax too."*

Here's how to plan it properly.

## Dubai visa for Indians (2026)

**Most Indian passport holders need a UAE tourist visa approved before they fly.** It's an e-visa — no embassy visit — and we apply for it as part of every Dubai package. Typical options are 30-day and 60-day tourist visas, single or multiple entry.

**Visa on arrival** is available only to some Indians: generally those holding a **valid US visa or green card**, or a residence permit/visa from countries such as the **UK or EU** (with conditions on validity). Don't assume you qualify — check before booking a one-way plan around it.

**Passport:** at least six months' validity. **Overstaying** is fined per day, with no grace period for tourist visas.

## Best time to visit Dubai

| Months | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| November – March | Best weather (pleasant days, cool evenings); Global Village and Miracle Garden usually open |
| April – May, October | Hot but manageable; good hotel deals |
| June – September | Very hot; great for indoor attractions and big summer sales |

Diwali and the winter school holidays are the busiest weeks for departures from Ahmedabad — book flights early.

## A 4N/5D Dubai itinerary that works for families

### Day 1 — Arrive and dhow cruise
Airport pick-up and hotel check-in. In the evening, a **dhow cruise with dinner** along Dubai Marina — an easy, relaxed first night.

### Day 2 — City tour and Burj Khalifa
Jumeirah Mosque (photo stop), **Dubai Frame**, the Palm and Atlantis, then **Burj Khalifa "At the Top"** at sunset. Finish at the Dubai Fountain show.

### Day 3 — Desert safari
A slow morning, then **dune bashing, camel rides, henna** and a **BBQ dinner** with live shows at a desert camp. Ask for vegetarian/Jain meals in advance.

### Day 4 — Abu Dhabi, or Miracle Garden and Global Village
Either a full-day **Abu Dhabi** trip to the **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque**, or stay in Dubai for **Miracle Garden** and **Global Village** when they're open.

### Day 5 — Shopping and fly home
Gold Souk, Dubai Mall or Mall of the Emirates before your airport drop.

## Money-saving tips

- **Fly mid-week** from Ahmedabad when fares are lower.
- **Choose a hotel near a metro station** — Dubai Metro is clean, cheap and fast.
- **Bundle attractions**: combo tickets for Burj Khalifa + aquarium or theme parks usually beat buying separately.
- **Carry a forex card**; cards are accepted almost everywhere.
