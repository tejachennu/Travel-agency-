Kerala is the trip our travellers describe as *"very smooth from start to end"* — green, gentle and easy to love. This 6-day route is the one we recommend most for families and couples from Gujarat.

> **Route:** Kochi → **Munnar** (2N) → **Thekkady** (1N) → **Alleppey houseboat** (1N) → **Kochi** (1N). Best between **September and March**.

## Day 1 — Kochi to Munnar
Land in Kochi and drive into the hills (about 4 hours), stopping at **Cheeyappara** and **Valara** waterfalls. Evening at leisure among the tea estates.

## Day 2 — Munnar
**Eravikulam National Park** (home of the Nilgiri tahr), **Mattupetty Dam**, **Echo Point** and the **Tea Museum**. Sunsets over the tea gardens are unforgettable.

## Day 3 — Thekkady
Drive to Thekkady (about 3 hours). **Spice plantation walk**, **boating on Periyar Lake**, and a **Kathakali** or **Kalaripayattu** show in the evening.

## Day 4 — Alleppey houseboat
Board your **houseboat** around noon and cruise the backwaters through villages and paddy fields. Lunch, snacks and dinner are cooked on board — tell us about vegetarian or Jain preferences.

## Day 5 — Kochi
Disembark after breakfast and explore **Fort Kochi**: the Chinese fishing nets, St. Francis Church, Jew Town and **Marine Drive** at sunset.

## Day 6 — Fly home
Shopping for spices and tea, then your airport drop.

## When to go

| Months | Why |
| --- | --- |
| September – March | Pleasant weather; ideal for hills and houseboats |
| April – May | Warm and humid; hill stations still nice |
| June – August | Monsoon: lush, dramatic and cheaper |

## Tips from our trips

- **Book houseboats early** for December–January.
- **Carry a light jacket** for Munnar evenings.
- **Motion-sickness tablets** help on the winding hill roads.
