## Why split your stay between Legian and Ubud?

Bali traffic is slow, so where you sleep matters more than how many places you see.

- **Legian (beach side)** puts you close to the airport, beach clubs, Seminyak's restaurants and the south-Bali sights like Uluwatu and Tanah Lot.
- **Ubud (jungle side)** is calm, green and romantic — rice terraces, waterfalls, spas and the pool villas Bali is famous for.

Starting at the beach and ending in a private pool villa gives your honeymoon a proper "grand finale".

## Day-by-day Bali honeymoon itinerary

### Day 1 — Arrive in Bali
Clear immigration (visa on arrival or e-VoA — see below), meet your driver and check in at Fairfield by Marriott Bali Legian. Keep the evening light: a walk on Legian beach and dinner in Seminyak.

### Day 2 — South Bali: water sports, Uluwatu and Dreamland
Morning water sports at Tanjung Benoa — banana boat, jet ski and fly-fish. After lunch, head to **Uluwatu temple** on its cliff above the Indian Ocean, then unwind at **Dreamland beach**.

### Day 3 — North Bali: the lake temple and waterfalls
A full-day tour to **Ulun Danu Beratan**, the temple that seems to float on Lake Beratan, the cascades of **Gitgit Waterfall**, and photos at the **Handara Gate**. Carry a light jacket — the highlands are cool.

### Day 4 — Bali Swing and Tanah Lot sunset
Swing over the jungle at **PicHeaven Bali Swing**, then drive to **Tanah Lot** for Bali's most famous sunset over a sea temple.

### Day 5 — Your day in Legian
Spa, beach club, café-hopping in Seminyak or simply the hotel pool. Couples often book a candle-lit dinner tonight.

### Day 6 — Transfer to your Ubud pool villa
Check in at **Dedary Resort & Spa, Ubud** — a one-bedroom villa with its own pool and bathtub. Order the famous floating breakfast for tomorrow.

### Day 7 — Kintamani and Ubud
See the **Tegalalang rice terraces**, taste Bali coffee at a **coffee plantation**, and browse **Ubud Market** for gifts. Optional add-ons couples love: an ATV ride or a sunrise jeep tour at Mount Batur.

### Day 8 — Fly home
Breakfast in your villa and transfer to the airport.

## Bali visa and arrival rules for Indians (2026)

- **Visa on arrival / e-VoA:** IDR 500,000 for **30 days**, extendable once by 30 days. Buying the **e-VoA online** before you fly saves time at the airport.
- **Bali tourist levy:** a separate **IDR 150,000 per person**, paid on the official **Love Bali** website or app.
- **Arrival card:** complete Indonesia's online **arrival declaration** shortly before landing (it's free on the official immigration site).
- **Passport:** at least six months' validity and blank pages.

We arrange the e-VoA and levy for our guests, but if you're doing it yourself, only use the official sites linked below.

## Best time for a Bali honeymoon

**April to October** is the dry season — sunny days for beaches and tours. July–August and Indian holiday weeks are busiest, so book early. **November to March** is greener, quieter and cheaper, with short afternoon showers.

## Budget tips that don't feel like compromises

- **Mix your hotels:** a quality beach hotel + two nights in a pool villa gives the luxury feel without seven nights of villa prices.
- **Book flights early** from Ahmedabad or Mumbai — fares jump close to peak months.
- **Use a private car with driver for tours** — it's often cheaper than separate taxis and far more comfortable.
- **Carry some Indonesian rupiah** for markets and tips; cards are widely accepted in hotels.
