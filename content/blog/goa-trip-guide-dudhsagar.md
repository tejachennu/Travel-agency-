Goa is India's favourite escape — sun, sand and *susegad*, the Goan art of taking life easy. Our travellers' "Goa Diaries" range from Dudhsagar's thundering falls to quiet Portuguese churches, and this plan covers both.

> **Quick plan:** **3N/4D** · stay in **North Goa** (or split with South) · **Dudhsagar** jeep safari in season · **Old Goa** churches · best **November–February**.

## North Goa or South Goa?

| | North Goa | South Goa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibe | Lively, shacks, nightlife | Calm, scenic, spacious |
| Beaches | Calangute, Baga, Anjuna, Vagator | Colva, Benaulim, Palolem, Agonda |
| Best for | Friends, first-timers | Couples, families wanting quiet |

## A 3N/4D Goa itinerary

### Day 1 — Arrive and Baga sunset
Check in and head to **Baga** or **Calangute** for a beach-shack sunset.

### Day 2 — North Goa
**Fort Aguada**, **Anjuna** and **Vagator** beaches, **Chapora Fort** views, and a **Mandovi river cruise** in the evening.

### Day 3 — Dudhsagar and Old Goa
A **jeep safari to Dudhsagar Falls** (seasonal), then the UNESCO-listed churches of **Old Goa** — the **Basilica of Bom Jesus** and **Se Cathedral**. Many trips add a spice plantation lunch.

### Day 4 — South Goa on the way out
If your flight allows, stop at **Colva** or **Palolem** before the airport.

## Dudhsagar Falls: what to know

- The falls tumble about **300 metres** down the Western Ghats on the Goa–Karnataka border.
- Visitors usually go by **authorised jeep safari** from Collem — the jeep road and pool area are typically closed in **peak monsoon** for safety.
- Wear **sturdy footwear**; the last stretch is a short walk over rocks.

## Tips

- **Rent a scooter only if you're confident** — or use a car with driver.
- **Book beach-front stays early** for Christmas and New Year.
- **Carry sunscreen and a hat** — the sun is strong even in winter.
