Singapore and Malaysia together are the dream "first big family trip" — two countries, short hops, theme parks for the kids and skylines for the parents. Our families come back with the happiest photos, Universal globe and Petronas Towers included.

> **Visa snapshot (2026):** **Singapore** — visa required for Indians (via authorised agents) + free **SG Arrival Card**. **Malaysia** — **visa-free up to 30 days until 31 December 2026** + free **MDAC**.

## The 6N/7D route

**Singapore (4 nights) → Kuala Lumpur & Genting (2 nights)**

### Day 1 — Arrive in Singapore
Marina Bay in the evening and the **Spectra** light-and-water show.

### Day 2 — City and Gardens by the Bay
Merlion Park, Chinatown and Little India, then **Flower Dome**, **Cloud Forest** and the **Supertree** light show.

### Day 3 — Sentosa and Universal Studios
A full day of rides at **Universal Studios Singapore**, then **Wings of Time** on the beach.

### Day 4 — Night Safari
A slow morning (Orchard Road or the hotel pool), then the **Night Safari** tram after dark.

### Day 5 — Singapore → Kuala Lumpur
A short flight or coach to KL. Evening at **KLCC park** below the **Petronas Twin Towers**.

### Day 6 — Batu Caves and Genting Highlands
The colourful steps of **Batu Caves**, then the **Awana Skyway** cable car to cool **Genting**.

### Day 7 — Fly home from Kuala Lumpur
Shopping at Bukit Bintang before the airport.

## Visa and arrival cards, explained

| | Singapore | Malaysia |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visa for Indians | Required (authorised agent) | Not required, up to 30 days (until 31 Dec 2026) |
| Arrival card | SG Arrival Card, free, within 3 days before arrival | MDAC, free, before arrival |
| Passport | 6+ months validity | 6+ months validity |

Use only the **official** arrival-card websites — copycat sites charge for free forms.

## Family tips

- **Buy theme park tickets online** for the best prices and to skip queues.
- **Singapore's MRT** is buggy-friendly and air-conditioned.
- **Plan a pool afternoon** — kids (and parents) need a break from the heat.
- **Vegetarian food** is easy in both countries, especially around Little India.
