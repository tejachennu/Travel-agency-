Vietnam has become one of the most loved destinations among our travellers — the Golden Bridge at Ba Na Hills, Ha Long Bay's limestone islands, lantern-lit Hoi An and excellent value for money. The only bit of homework is the visa, and it's easy once you know the steps.

> **Quick facts (2026):** Indians need a **Vietnam e-visa**. It's valid for up to **90 days**, **single (USD 25)** or **multiple entry (USD 50)**, and usually takes **3–5 working days**. Apply on the **official portal** only.

## Step-by-step: applying for the Vietnam e-visa

1. **Open the official e-visa portal** (linked below). Many lookalike sites charge two or three times the real fee.
2. **Choose single or multiple entry** and your intended entry date and validity.
3. **Upload a passport-style photo** (white background, no glasses) and a clear **scan of your passport's data page**.
4. **Fill in your details** — address in India, occupation, hotel address in Vietnam and your planned port of entry.
5. **Pay the fee online** by card and note your **registration code**.
6. **Check status** with the code and email; **download and print** the e-visa when approved.

## Documents checklist

- Passport valid for **6+ months** with blank pages
- Digital passport photo (4×6 format, white background)
- Passport data-page scan
- Hotel address (first night is enough)
- Return or onward flight booking

## Which entry to choose?

Choose **multiple entry** if you'll cross into Cambodia or Laos and return, or if you're combining Vietnam with another country on the same trip. For a straightforward Hanoi–Ha Long–Da Nang–Hoi An holiday, **single entry** is enough.

## Common mistakes

- **Name order wrong** — enter it exactly as your passport's machine-readable zone.
- **Blurry passport scan** — leads to rejection or delays.
- **Wrong entry date** — you can't enter before the date on your visa.
- **Paying an unofficial site** — always check the URL ends in **.gov.vn**.
