Kashmir in winter is a completely different trip from the summer postcards: quieter, colder and quietly magical. One of our most-watched reels shows a shikara gliding through a frozen, misty Dal Lake, and it sums up the season perfectly. For travellers from Gujarat who have never seen real snow, there's nothing quite like it.

> **The short version:** Go between **late December and February** for reliable snow (early March for milder weather). Base yourself in **Srinagar**, spend a day or night in **Gulmarg** for the **Gondola** (book online on the official JKCCC site, with photo ID), add **Pahalgam** or **Sonamarg**, and plan **5–6 nights with one buffer day** for snow and flight delays. Pack thermals, a proper down jacket and boots with grip.

## Kashmir in winter: month by month

| Month | Weather and snow | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early–mid December | Cold, dry, first snow in Gulmarg possible | Quieter, better hotel rates |
| Late December | Chillai Kalan (the harshest 40 days) begins around 21 December | Christmas and New Year snow trips |
| January | Coldest month; Srinagar nights well below zero; peak snow in the hills | Skiing, frozen edges of Dal Lake |
| February | Still snowy in Gulmarg and Pahalgam; days slowly warming | Best balance of snow and comfort |
| March | Snow stays up high; Srinagar thaws | Gentler trip, first spring colours late in the month |

Chillai Kalan runs from about 21 December to the end of January. This is when you're most likely to see icicles, a partly frozen Dal Lake and the heaviest snowfall. It's also when flights and roads are most likely to be disrupted.

## Getting there from Ahmedabad

**By air:** IndiGo has operated a nonstop Ahmedabad–Srinagar flight of roughly 2 hours 15 minutes, though how often it runs changes by season. Otherwise, connect via Delhi or Mumbai. Winter fog in Delhi and snow at Srinagar can delay flights, so leave **at least 3–4 hours** between connecting flights on separate tickets and avoid booking an onward flight on the same day you leave Srinagar.

**By train:** the rail line now reaches Srinagar from the Jammu side, but for a short winter holiday from Gujarat, flying in is still far quicker.

## Where to go in winter

### Srinagar

Your base. Expect misty mornings over **Dal Lake**, shikaras at the ghats on the Boulevard, and the Mughal gardens (Nishat, Shalimar) bare and frosted rather than in bloom. In the old city, see the **Jamia Masjid**, the wooden **Khanqah-e-Moula** from outside, and the markets for pashmina, papier-mâché, walnuts and saffron. A short shikara ride in the late morning, once the fog lifts, is the classic winter moment.

### Gulmarg and the Gondola

About 50 km (1.5–2 hours) from Srinagar, Gulmarg is the most reliable place for snow in Kashmir and India's best-known ski resort, with the season running roughly from late December to early March. The final 13 km from **Tangmarg** climbs steeply and can be slow or briefly blocked after heavy snowfall; cabs need snow chains, and some drivers try to make you change vehicles at Tangmarg, so confirm your fare covers the full journey.

The **Gulmarg Gondola** runs in two phases:

| Phase | Route | Approx. top altitude | Who it suits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | Gulmarg to Kongdoori | About 3,050 m | Everyone: snow play, sledging, views |
| Phase 2 | Kongdoori to Apharwat | Close to 4,000 m | Fit adults, skiers; frequently suspended in wind, cloud or heavy snow |

**How booking works (as of late 2026):**

- Book **online** on the official Jammu and Kashmir Cable Car Corporation website (linked in the sources). On-the-spot counter sales were suspended in 2025, so don't plan on buying at the base station.
- Tickets carry the visitor's name, and you must show **matching original photo ID** (Aadhaar, passport or driving licence) at the gate.
- Both phases are sold in time slots. Phase 2 has far fewer seats than Phase 1, and you need a Phase 1 ticket to ride it.
- A small **Tatkal quota** for the next day opens online in the evening at higher fares.
- Fares change from season to season, and Phase 2 costs more than Phase 1, so check the current price on the official site rather than trusting old blog figures.
- Ignore touts outside the base station offering "confirmed" tickets.

For most families, **Phase 1 is enough**. Kongdoori has plenty of snow, sledge rides and space for kids, and you're spared the altitude and the weather lottery of Phase 2. Book Phase 1 for an early slot: the lifts are calmer and the light is best in the morning.

### Pahalgam

About 95 km (2.5–3 hours) from Srinagar, along the Lidder river. In winter, Pahalgam is a snow-covered village with pine forests and very few tourists. Local union taxis take visitors to the valleys around it: **Betaab Valley** and **Aru Valley**, depending on snow and access that day. Several Pahalgam spots were closed after April 2025 and reopened in phases through 2025 and early 2026; access to some meadows, such as Baisaran, has been restricted at times. Check the current status with J&K Tourism or your local driver before you go.

### Sonamarg

About 80 km from Srinagar. Sonamarg used to be cut off for most of winter, but the **Z-Morh (Sonamarg) tunnel**, opened in January 2025, now gives year-round road access in most conditions. Avalanche warnings can still close the road, so treat it as a weather-dependent day trip with **Thajiwas Glacier** and snow play as the highlights.

## A 6-day Kashmir winter itinerary

| Day | Plan | Overnight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Fly to Srinagar; check into a heated hotel or houseboat; short shikara ride if calm | Srinagar |
| 2 | Mughal gardens, old city, local markets | Srinagar |
| 3 | Drive to Gulmarg; Gondola Phase 1; snow play or a ski lesson | Gulmarg (or back to Srinagar) |
| 4 | Drive to Pahalgam; Betaab or Aru Valley by local taxi, as access allows | Pahalgam |
| 5 | Return to Srinagar via saffron fields at Pampore; shopping | Srinagar |
| 6 | Fly home (morning flights are most likely to be delayed by fog; keep a buffer) | — |

## Where to stay

- **Houseboats on Dal or Nigeen Lake:** the romantic choice. In winter, ask specifically about **central heating or electric blankets**; older boats with only a bukhari (wood stove) can be very cold at night.
- **Hotels in Srinagar** around the Boulevard, Rajbagh and Sonwar: easier for seniors and families, with heating and hot water.
- **Gulmarg:** a small number of hotels, most of them busy and expensive in ski season. Book well ahead if you want to sleep in the snow.
- **Pahalgam:** hotels along the Lidder river; check heating and power backup.

What drives the cost: Christmas–New Year and February long weekends, heating standards, Gulmarg overnight stays, cab type (an SUV with snow chains costs more but is worth it) and flight fares, which jump around holidays.

## Food: vegetarian and Jain options

Traditional Kashmiri **wazwan** is meat-heavy, but vegetarians eat very well. Look for **dum aloo (Kashmiri style)**, **nadru yakhni** (lotus stem in yoghurt gravy), **rajma with rice**, **haak saag** and **chaman (paneer)**. Warm up with **kahwa** (saffron and almond green tea) and try **noon chai** (pink salt tea) once. Srinagar has many pure-veg restaurants serving North Indian and Gujarati-style food.

Jain travellers should tell hotels and houseboats in advance; no-onion, no-garlic cooking is possible but not standard. Carry **thepla, khakhra and dry snacks** for long drives and Gulmarg, where choices are limited.

## What to wear and pack

- **Thermal innerwear** (two sets, top and bottom) and **woollen socks**
- A proper **down or padded jacket**; a sweater alone is not enough
- **Waterproof gloves, woollen cap, muffler** and sunglasses for snow glare
- **Boots with a deep-grip sole**; you can also rent snow boots and long coats in Gulmarg and Pahalgam
- **Moisturiser, lip balm and sunscreen**, as the cold air is very dry
- **Power bank**, because phone batteries drain fast in the cold
- Note: **prepaid SIMs from other states don't work in J&K**. Use a postpaid connection.

## Who it suits

- **Families with kids:** sledging at Kongdoori and snowball fights are the highlight. Keep days short and warm.
- **Couples:** misty shikara rides and a Gulmarg night make a memorable honeymoon.
- **Seniors:** manageable with a slower plan, heated rooms, Phase 1 only and good footwear for icy paths. Check with a doctor if anyone has heart or breathing issues.
- **Skiers:** Gulmarg has ski schools and equipment rental for first-timers; serious skiers go for Phase 2 terrain.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **No buffer day.** Snow closes roads and delays flights; one free day saves the trip.
- **Leaving Gondola booking till you arrive.** Don't rely on a counter; book online as soon as your dates are fixed.
- **Buying "Gondola tickets" from touts.** Your name must match your ID.
- **Assuming every houseboat is heated.** Ask before booking.
- **Ignoring advisories.** Follow local road and weather updates from J&K authorities and your driver.
