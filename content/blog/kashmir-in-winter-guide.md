Kashmir in winter is a completely different trip from the summer postcards — quieter, colder and quietly magical. One of our most-watched reels shows a shikara gliding through a frozen, misty Dal Lake, and it sums up the season perfectly.

> **Winter in short:** **December–February** · snow in **Gulmarg** and **Pahalgam** · cold, misty **Srinagar** · short days · pack serious warm clothes · keep one buffer day for weather.

## What to expect month by month

| Month | What it's like |
| --- | --- |
| December | First good snowfall in Gulmarg; festive, cold |
| January | Coldest month ("Chillai Kalan"); best chance of a partly frozen Dal Lake; peak snow |
| February | Still snowy in the hills, slowly warming in Srinagar |

Roads to higher areas can close after heavy snowfall, so build in flexibility.

## A 6-day Kashmir winter itinerary

1. **Srinagar** — check into a heated houseboat; short shikara ride if the weather allows.
2. **Srinagar** — Mughal gardens in their winter coat and the old city.
3. **Gulmarg (day trip or overnight)** — **gondola** to Kongdori, snow activities, skiing lessons.
4. **Pahalgam** — **Betaab Valley** and **Aru Valley** in the snow (local cabs in winter).
5. **Srinagar** — shopping for pashmina, kahwa and dry fruits.
6. **Fly home** from Srinagar.

## Packing list for snow

- **Thermals** (top and bottom) and **woollen socks**
- **Down jacket**, **gloves**, **cap**, **muffler**
- **Waterproof boots** — or rent snow boots in Gulmarg
- **Moisturiser and lip balm** — the cold air is very dry
- **Power bank** — batteries drain fast in the cold

## Tips from our desk

- **Book a houseboat or hotel with good heating** — ask specifically.
- **Gondola tickets** sell out in peak snow weeks; book in advance.
- **Morning flights** into Srinagar can be delayed by fog — avoid tight connections.
- **Respect local advisories** on road closures.
