The Sahyadri hill stations make an ideal family trip from Gujarat: they're close, green and full of viewpoints, and magical in the monsoon. This Matheran, Lonavala and Mahabaleshwar itinerary links all three with a day at Imagicaa. It's the route our travellers loved, with *"a great experience at every place."*

> **The short version:** In 5N/6D, go Mumbai (1N) → **Matheran** (1N) → **Lonavala** (2N, with a day at **Imagicaa**) → **Panchgani & Mahabaleshwar** (1N), then head home from Pune or Mumbai. The best months are **October–February** for clear views and **July–September** for waterfalls. Matheran is **car-free**, and the Neral toy train **pauses every monsoon**.

## Getting there from Gujarat

- **Train to Mumbai:** the Vande Bharat and Shatabdi trains from Ahmedabad take roughly 5.5–7 hours, and there are plenty of overnight trains if you want to arrive fresh in the morning.
- **Fly:** Ahmedabad–Mumbai is a short hop of around 1 hour 15 minutes. Ending in **Pune** is often smarter, because it's much closer to Mahabaleshwar and nonstop Pune–Ahmedabad flights have operated.
- **Drive:** about 530 km to Mumbai, but most families prefer to take the train and use a local cab with a driver for the hills.

## Day-by-day plan

### Day 1: Aamchi Mumbai

Arrive in Mumbai and see the **Gateway of India**, **Marine Drive** at sunset and **Juhu** beach. If you're arriving by overnight train, you could skip Mumbai and drive straight to Matheran, which buys you a night in the hills.

### Day 2: Matheran

Drive to **Dasturi Naka**, the car park at the edge of town. From here you continue on foot, horseback, e-rickshaw or the toy train shuttle (details below). Spend the afternoon on Matheran's red-earth paths:

- **Echo Point** and **Charlotte Lake**, a short walk from each other
- **Louisa Point** for wide views across to Prabal Fort
- **Panorama Point** at sunrise and **Porcupine (Sunset) Point** in the evening
- **One Tree Hill** and **Alexander Point** if you still have energy

Stay overnight in Matheran itself. It's when the day-trippers leave that the town is at its best.

### Day 3: Lonavala & Khandala

Drive down via Karjat and Khopoli to Lonavala. See **Tiger Point (Tiger's Leap)** and **Lion's Point** for valley views, **Bhushi Dam** (best in monsoon), and the roughly 2,000-year-old Buddhist rock-cut **Karla Caves**. Karla involves a steep climb of a few hundred steps, and a small ASI entry fee applies. Nearby **Bhaja Caves** and **Lohagad Fort** suit active families. Finish with **chikki** and **fudge** shopping in Lonavala market.

### Day 4: Imagicaa

Spend the day at **Imagicaa** theme park near Khopoli, about 40 minutes from Lonavala. Adult tickets have recently been listed at around ₹1,300 on weekdays and ₹1,500 at weekends, and a price rise has been reported for late 2026, so check the official site. The **snow park** is an add-on. Imagicaa's **water park** was temporarily closed from 19 June 2026 because of water restrictions, so confirm it has reopened before planning a water-park day. Go on a **weekday** and buy tickets online for shorter queues. Check ride height limits for younger children.

### Day 5: Panchgani & Mahabaleshwar

Drive via Pune and Wai to **Panchgani** for **Table Land**, **Sydney Point** and **Parsi Point**, then stop at **Mapro Garden** for strawberry treats on the way to Mahabaleshwar. In the evening go boating on **Venna Lake**, which is closed in the monsoon. Next morning, if time allows, add **Arthur's Seat**, **Elephant's Head (Needle Hole) Point**, **Kate's Point** and the old **Mahabaleshwar temple**. **Pratapgad Fort**, built by Shivaji Maharaj, is about an hour's drive away and a favourite with history-minded travellers.

### Day 6: Head home

Drive to **Pune** (about 3 hours) or **Mumbai** (5.5–6.5 hours) for your train or flight. Leave a buffer, because weekend traffic on the ghats can be slow.

## Matheran's vehicle ban and toy train (2026)

### No cars beyond Dasturi

Matheran is an eco-sensitive zone and no private vehicles are allowed past **Dasturi Naka**. Pay for parking there, and pay the small municipal entry fee per person. From Dasturi to the main market area is roughly 2.5–3 km. Your options:

- **Walk:** about 45 minutes on mud paths. Porters will carry luggage for a fee.
- **Horse:** fun for children. Agree the rate before you start.
- **E-rickshaw:** after a 2025 Supreme Court order, **hand-pulled rickshaws are being phased out** and replaced by battery e-rickshaws. Their numbers are limited and services were disrupted by a strike in early 2026, so don't rely on getting one at peak times.

**For seniors**, we arrange a hotel near the market and plan horse or shuttle-train transfers in advance.

### The toy train

The **Matheran Hill Railway** runs a narrow-gauge line about 21 km from **Neral** up to Matheran, a slow and scenic ride of roughly 2–2.5 hours.

- **Monsoon suspension:** regular Neral–Matheran services stop every monsoon for safety. In 2026 the suspension began in mid-June and was notified until **15 October**. In both 2024 and 2025, full services resumed on **6 November**.
- **Aman Lodge shuttle:** the short shuttle between **Aman Lodge** (near Dasturi) and Matheran usually keeps running through the monsoon. It's the easiest way into town for children and seniors.
- **Tickets:** seats are limited and weekends sell out. Check the latest timetable with Central Railway or at Neral station before you go.

## Best time to visit

| Season | Good for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June – September | Waterfalls, mist, green valleys | Slippery roads, crowded weekends, toy train paused, Venna boating stopped |
| October – February | Clear views, pleasant days, strawberries from December | Peak-season hotel prices around Diwali and Christmas |
| March – May | Strawberry season early on, fewer crowds | Warm afternoons |

In heavy-rain spells, the Pune district administration sometimes issues orders restricting access to spots like Tiger Point and Bhushi Dam. Stay out of fast-flowing water and follow police instructions.

## Where to stay

- **Mumbai:** near the airport (Andheri, Juhu) for an easy exit, or South Mumbai for sightseeing.
- **Matheran:** close to the market and Charlotte Lake, to cut down on walking.
- **Lonavala:** in or near town for food and shopping, or around Khandala and Tungarli for views.
- **Mahabaleshwar:** near Venna Lake and the market for convenience, or **Panchgani** for quieter, cheaper stays.

## What drives the cost

- **Season and weekends:** Diwali, Christmas–New Year and monsoon weekends push hotel rates up the most.
- **Hotel category:** heritage and valley-view properties in Matheran and Mahabaleshwar cost more.
- **Vehicle:** a sedan works for a couple, while an Innova or Tempo Traveller suits families and groups.
- **Imagicaa tickets, horses and porters** add up for big families, so budget for them.
- **Start and end points:** flying into Mumbai and out of Pune saves a long drive back.

## Vegetarian and Jain food

This circuit is one of the easiest in India for Gujarati travellers. Matheran and Mahabaleshwar hotels see many Gujarati families, and **Gujarati thalis and Jain meals** are widely available on request. Try **vada pav** and **chikki** in Lonavala, **strawberries with cream** and fresh juices in Mahabaleshwar, and **corn pattice** in Mahabaleshwar. Ask for Jain food when you book, because some smaller Matheran hotels need notice.

## Packing list

- **Proper rain gear** in monsoon: umbrellas are no match for Sahyadri rain
- **Shoes with grip** for Matheran's mud paths and Karla's steps
- A **light jacket** for Mahabaleshwar evenings from November to February
- A small daypack, so you aren't hauling suitcases into Matheran
- Swimwear for Imagicaa's water park, if it's open

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Bringing big suitcases to Matheran.** Pack an overnight bag and leave the rest in the car.
- **Visiting Imagicaa at the weekend** if you can go on a weekday.
- **Assuming the toy train runs from Neral** in June to early November.
- **Doing Mahabaleshwar as a day trip from Lonavala.** It's too far, so stay at least one night.
- **Driving the ghats at night** in the monsoon.

## Who this itinerary suits

- **Families with kids:** horses, Imagicaa, boating and strawberry farms.
- **Seniors:** doable, with a market-side Matheran hotel and pre-booked transfers.
- **Couples:** add a second night in Mahabaleshwar and skip Imagicaa.
