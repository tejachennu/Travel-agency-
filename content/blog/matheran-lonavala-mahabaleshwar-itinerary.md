The Sahyadri hill stations are the perfect family trip from Gujarat: close, green, full of viewpoints — and in the monsoon, magical. This is the route our travellers loved, with *"a great experience at every place."*

> **Route:** Mumbai → **Matheran** → **Lonavala & Khandala** → **Imagica** → **Panchgani & Mahabaleshwar**. **5N/6D**. Best **June–February**.

## Day 1 — Aamchi Mumbai
Arrive in Mumbai. **Gateway of India**, **Marine Drive** at sunset and **Juhu** beach.

## Day 2 — Matheran
Drive to **Dasturi**, then walk or ride into car-free **Matheran**. **Echo Point**, **Louisa Point** and **Charlotte Lake**. The **toy train** from Neral runs seasonally.

## Day 3 — Lonavala & Khandala
**Tiger's Leap**, **Bhushi Dam** (best in monsoon), **Karla Caves** and Lonavala's famous chikki.

## Day 4 — Imagica
A full day at **Imagica** theme park — or **SnoMagica** for snow play.

## Day 5 — Panchgani & Mahabaleshwar
**Table Land** in Panchgani, then **Venna Lake**, **Arthur's Seat** and **strawberry farms** in Mahabaleshwar.

## Day 6 — Head home
Drive back to Mumbai or Pune for your train or flight.

## Monsoon or winter?

| Season | Good for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June – September | Waterfalls, mist, green valleys | Slippery roads, crowded weekends |
| October – February | Clear views, pleasant days | Peak-season hotel prices |
| March – May | Strawberry season early on, fewer crowds | Warm afternoons |

## Tips for families

- **Stay two nights in the hills** rather than one to avoid repeated packing.
- **Book Imagica tickets online** on weekdays for shorter queues.
- **Carry rain gear** in monsoon — umbrellas are no match for Sahyadri rain.
